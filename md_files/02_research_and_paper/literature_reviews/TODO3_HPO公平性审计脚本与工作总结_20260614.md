## 4. HPO 预算审计

结果文件：

```text
publication_eval_20260614/hpo_audit/hpo_budget_table.csv
publication_eval_20260614/hpo_audit/hpo_time_cost.csv
```

关键结果：

| Model | Trials | Startup trials | Elapsed | Seconds / trial |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| randomforest | 40 | 8 | 48.1m | 72.159 |
| xgboost | 50 | 8 | 16.1m | 19.348 |
| lightgbm | 50 | 8 | 11.4m | 13.734 |
| catboost | 50 | 8 | 38.6m | 46.284 |
| mlp | 30 | 8 | 15.9m | 31.757 |
| lstm | 20 | 8 | 10.35h | 1862.454 |
| wavenet | 20 | 8 | 18.41h | 3313.746 |
| 2dcnn | 20 | 8 | 38.73h | 6971.501 |

公平性结论：

- trial 数不相等：20 到 50 trials。
- startup/total 比例对低 trial 深度模型较高：LSTM/WaveNet/2D CNN 为 8/20 = 0.40。
- wall-clock 差异极大：最短 LightGBM 约 11.4 分钟，最长 2D CNN 约 38.73 小时。
- 这说明论文不能简单说“所有模型公平调参后比较”，必须写清楚两类预算：
  - trial budget
  - wall-clock / compute budget

推荐论文表述：

```text
All models were tuned with Optuna TPE under a fixed validation protocol.
The trial budgets were model-dependent due to large computational differences;
therefore, HPO budget and wall-clock cost are reported explicitly, and final
claims are based on frozen-hyperparameter multi-seed locked-test evaluation.
```

不建议表述：

```text
All models were tuned under identical budgets.
```

因为这不符合当前事实。

## 9. 当前不能用于论文的强结论

当前 HPO 审计不支持以下结论：

- “所有模型在完全相同预算下比较。”
- “验证集 best value 就是最终泛化性能。”
- “2D CNN 已经在测试集上显著优于所有模型。”
- “深度模型的优势已经排除了调参预算和随机性的影响。”

这些结论需要：

- TODO5：固定 best params 后多 seed 独立重训。
- TODO6：locked test 主指标表。
- TODO8：paired bootstrap / Wilcoxon 统计检验。
- TODO13：复杂度和成本报告。

1. Akiba, T., Sano, S., Yanase, T., Ohta, T., & Koyama, M. (2019). Optuna: A next-generation hyperparameter optimization framework. *KDD 2019*. [ACM DOI](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3292500.3330701), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.10902)
   - 对应规范：说明当前 HPO 工具和 TPE/define-by-run 框架来源。

2. Sivaprasad, P. T., Mai, F., Vogels, T., Jaggi, M., & Fleuret, F. (2020). Optimizer benchmarking needs to account for hyperparameter tuning. *ICML*. [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/sivaprasad20a.html)
   - 对应规范：比较优化/模型时必须考虑超参数搜索成本和预算。

3. Schmidt, R. M., Schneider, F., & Hennig, P. (2021). Descending through a crowded valley: Benchmarking deep learning optimizers. *ICML*. [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/schmidt21a.html)
   - 对应规范：大规模 benchmark 需要标准化实验协议、预算和多运行证据。

4. Erickson, N., Purucker, L., Tschalzev, A., Holzmueller, D., Desai, P. M., Salinas, D., & Hutter, F. (2025). TabArena: A living benchmark for machine learning on tabular data. *NeurIPS 2025 Datasets and Benchmarks*. [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=jZqCqpCLdU), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16791)
   - 对应规范：表格 benchmark 需要报告 validation method、HPO、ensembling、模型适用范围和计算预算。

5. Cawley, G. C., & Talbot, N. L. C. (2010). On over-fitting in model selection and subsequent selection bias in performance evaluation. *Journal of Machine Learning Research*. [JMLR](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html)
   - 对应规范：HPO 选择过程会引入模型选择偏差，最终性能必须依赖独立 locked test。
